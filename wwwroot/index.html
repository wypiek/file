A few things to know

Files are stored on this host until its administrator removes them. Keep a backup of anything important. Links are unlisted, but they are not password protected.

Already compressed files, such as ZIPs, JPEGs, and videos, may not get smaller. Downloads always return the original file. Image previews are available for recognized PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP files up to 20 MB. Discord controls whether and when a preview appears.
